I keep a stack of A5 dot-grid paper next to my keyboard. Every project starts there, and I try to stay there until the sketches stop changing.

## Fidelity is a commitment

The moment a screen has real type and real colour, people react to the type and the colour. Sketches keep the conversation about the structure.

## Volume over polish

In a sketching hour I can produce twenty layouts. In Figma I produce three, and I get attached to the first one.

## When to stop

When two consecutive sketching sessions produce the same layout, it is time to open the tool. Not before.
