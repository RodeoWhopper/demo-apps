About us

Started in a fish warehouse. Still smells like coffee, mostly.

Nordwind began in 2019 when two friends bought a second-hand 12 kg Probat and a lease on a draughty warehouse on Bryggen. We still roast there.

Timeline

Seven years, one roaster.

  • 2019First roast on the Probat. First customers: the café next door.
  • 2020Subscriptions launch. We ship 400 bags in December.
  • 2022First direct-trade contract signed in Huila, Colombia.
  • 2024Wholesale programme opens to cafés across western Norway.
  • 2026Fourteen partner farms. Still the same roaster, freshly re-bricked.
