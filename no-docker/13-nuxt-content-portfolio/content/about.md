I'm a product designer splitting my time between Istanbul and Berlin. For the last eleven years I've worked mostly on business software — logistics dashboards, fintech onboarding, internal tools that thousands of people use every day without ever loving them. My job is to make that software quieter.

Before design I studied industrial engineering, which is probably why I keep reaching for flow diagrams and why I'm comfortable in a room full of backend engineers. I've led design systems at two scale-ups, taught a design-for-developers course at a bootcamp, and I still take one small product project a year just to remember how it feels to ship alone.

## Outside work

I run slowly, bake bread badly and collect old Turkish typography specimen books. If you have one from before 1960, please write to me.
