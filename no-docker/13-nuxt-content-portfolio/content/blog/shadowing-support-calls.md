Every discovery phase I run starts the same way: I ask the support lead for a headset and two days.

## Why it works

Interview participants are polite. Support callers are not. They describe the product as it actually behaves, using the words they actually use, at the exact moment it failed them. You cannot buy that signal.

## How to do it well

1. Listen only. Do not take over calls.
2. Write down phrases verbatim. "The blue thing" is data.
3. Tally categories in a spreadsheet as you go; by the end of day two the shape of the problem is usually visible.
4. Thank the team publicly. They will keep sending you things.

## What you get

On the Harbor project, two days on the line produced the phrase "the screen is noise" that ended up framing the whole redesign. No interview would have given me that so quickly.
