## How I work

Most of my projects start with a week of listening: shadowing support calls, reading tickets, sitting with the people who actually use the product. I sketch in low fidelity for as long as I can get away with, then move to a component library so the last mile is shared with engineering instead of handed over.

I care about three things above everything else: **clarity** (people should never wonder what a button does), **consistency** (a system beats a hundred beautiful one-offs) and **pace** (a shipped 80% teaches more than a perfect mock-up).
